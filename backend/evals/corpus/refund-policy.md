# Refund Policy

**Last updated: 2026-01-15**

## Standard Products

Customers may request a full refund within **30 days** of purchase for any reason.
After 30 days, refunds are granted only for defective products.

To start a refund, email support@example.com with your order number and reason.
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

## Digital Goods

Digital downloads and software licences are **non-refundable** once the download
link has been accessed or the licence key has been activated.

Exception: if the digital product is demonstrably defective (crashes on launch,
wrong file format), a replacement or store credit is offered within 14 days.

## Subscription Plans

Paid subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Access continues until the end
of the current billing period. No partial refunds are issued for unused time
within a paid period.

Annual plans cancelled within the first 7 days receive a full refund.

## Bundles

If a bundle is partially used, the refund amount is the bundle price minus the
retail price of the items already downloaded or activated.

## Contact

Refund requests that fall outside these terms are reviewed case-by-case by the
billing team. Response time is 1–3 business days.
