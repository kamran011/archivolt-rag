# Pricing

**Effective: 2026-04-01**

## Plans

### Starter — Free

- Up to **3 knowledge bases**
- Up to **500 MB** of indexed content
- **50 answers per month**
- Community support only

### Pro — $49/month (or $470/year, save 20%)

- Up to **20 knowledge bases**
- Up to **10 GB** of indexed content
- **2 000 answers per month**
- Email support (response within 1 business day)
- Retrieval debugger and evaluation lab

### Business — $199/month (or $1 910/year, save 20%)

- **Unlimited** knowledge bases
- Up to **100 GB** of indexed content
- **20 000 answers per month**
- Priority support (response within 4 hours)
- SSO/SAML, audit logs, and custom retention

### Enterprise — Custom pricing

- Unlimited everything
- Dedicated infrastructure option
- SLA with 99.9% uptime guarantee
- Onboarding and integration support

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price |
|--------|-------|
| Extra 10 GB storage | $15/month |
| Extra 5 000 answers | $30/month |
| Web-search fallback (per 1 000 queries) | $5 |

## Overages

Answers above plan limits are billed at **$0.015 per answer**. Storage overages
block new uploads; existing knowledge bases remain queryable.

## Billing

Subscriptions renew automatically. Cancel at any time in Settings → Billing.
Annual plans are non-refundable after the 7-day trial period. Monthly plans
can be cancelled any time; access continues to end of billing period.

## Free Trial

All paid plans include a **14-day free trial**, no credit card required.
At trial end, downgrade to Starter automatically or choose a paid plan.
