# Shipping Policy

**Last updated: 2026-01-15**

## Domestic Shipping (US)

| Method | Estimated Delivery | Cost |
|--------|-------------------|------|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $4.99 |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $24.99 |

Orders placed before 2 PM ET ship the same day. Orders after 2 PM ET ship the
next business day. Orders are not processed on weekends or US federal holidays.

Free standard shipping applies to orders over **$50**.

## International Shipping

We ship to over 40 countries. International orders use DHL Express (5–10 business
days, cost varies by weight and destination).

Import duties and taxes are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in the
shipping charge shown at checkout.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Tracking updates may take up to
24 hours to appear after the order leaves the warehouse.

## Lost or Damaged Packages

Report a lost or damaged package within 30 days of the estimated delivery date.
We will file a carrier claim and either reship the order or issue a refund at
our discretion.

## PO Boxes and APO/FPO Addresses

We ship to PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses via USPS Priority Mail only.
Expedited and overnight options are not available for these destinations.
